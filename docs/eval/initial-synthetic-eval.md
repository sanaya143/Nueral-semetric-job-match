# Initial evaluation on the synthetic dataset

Mode: **offline** · 64 CVs · 7 job descriptions · total model cost **$0.0000** · ingestion 2.52s. Generated by `scripts/evaluate.py`; rerun it after any change to extraction, retrieval or scoring.

> **Read this first.** The gold labels come from the synthetic generator's ground truth, not from recruiters. The generator and the engine share a view of what "evidence" means, so a good result here is necessary, not sufficient. It shows the pipeline works and the core claim holds on controlled data. Real-world quality must be measured on a recruiter-graded, consented design-partner set (Phase 2 exit gate).

## What this does not show

- Real CV variety (scans, multi-column PDFs, regional formats, non-English text).
- Recruiter agreement: grades here are rule-based, not human judgements.
- LLM-mode quality and cost: run `scripts/evaluate.py --use-env` with an OpenRouter key to measure them (the gateway enforces the $0.20/run cap).
- Scale: 64 CVs. Latency at 100K+ candidates needs the pgvector build (ADR 0009).
